This branch is the public SkillForge release. It contains the SkillForge core,
CPU reference loop, optional trainer integration, benchmark adapters, and
teacher-guided skill generators. The trainer, rollout, environment, memory,
and GiGPO components retain their source-file license and copyright notices;
see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for the directory-level summary.

Private provider endpoints, company SDKs, credentials, datasets, machine paths,
and experiment-only launchers are not included. Runtime dependencies and model
or environment resources are supplied by the caller.
